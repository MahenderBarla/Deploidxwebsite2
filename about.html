Our story

Who we are

Super AI Academy is a training institute focused on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Generative AI. We help students, working professionals and business owners understand AI and use it to solve real problems.

Our courses are designed by industry practitioners and updated regularly so that learners always work with current tools and techniques.

“Learn by building.”

Every concept we teach is paired with a practical exercise or project.
